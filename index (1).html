How to play

  1. Every face of the cube is its own game of noughts and crosses, so you're playing six games at once.
  2. On your turn, swipe the cube to roll it (or press Roll). Put your counter on a free square of the face that lands in front of you. If it lands on a face that's already finished, bad luck: your turn is over. As faces fill up, the cube lands on unfinished faces more often, so games don't drag.
  3. Three in a row wins that face and locks it. A full face with no line is a draw.
  4. When all six faces are done, whoever has won the most faces wins the game.
  5. In Pick it mode there's no rolling: swipe to turn the cube one side at a time, or tap a face on the map, then play wherever you like.
  6. In Stack it mode you share one 3×3×3 cube. Swipe to turn it, then tap an empty cell. Win with three in a row on a level, straight up a column, or diagonally up through the cube. Tap Layers to see the three floors flat — handy for the middle cell. The very centre is locked for the first two rounds — it's the strongest cell, so everyone gets an equal shot at it.
  7. Beat Robo (tap it on the main screen): play Robo alone. Level 1 is three in a row, then four, five and six, and the grid grows with each one. In Roll it, every face scores: 3 for a win, 1 each for a draw, and −1 for a loss. Most points takes the level, and a tie goes to one sudden-death face. In Stack it, make the line. You start with 3 hearts and lose one for each level you lose. Lose them all and it's game over. Clear every level to loop round for a bonus heart, with a sharper Robo and multiplied points. Your score builds as you go, and when the run ends you can post it with your initials to the Roll it or Stack it leaderboard, this week or all time. Progress is never saved, so every run starts at Level 1.
  8. To play online, one person taps Host online and shares the four-letter room code. Everyone else types the code and taps Join on their own device. The host picks the mode, can add bots, and starts the game. If someone drops out, a bot takes their seat.
  9. On a keyboard, the arrow keys roll or turn the cube, Space rolls, and Tab then Enter places a counter.
  10. Prefer it darker? Flip the Dark mode switch at the top of the main screen. It's remembered next time.

Robo is the champion of the cube, and he isn't giving up his crown without a fight. Take him on one to one and climb the levels. Every level the grid grows and you need one more in a row to win, so three becomes four, then five, then six. Robo gets sharper as you go, too.

  • You get 3 hearts. Lose a level and you lose a heart, then have another go. Lose them all and it's game over.
  • Clear every level and you loop round: a bonus heart (up to 5), a sharper Robo, and your points multiplied by the loop.
  • No saves. Like an old arcade cabinet, every run starts back at Level 1. When it ends, put your initials on the leaderboard.
  • You play as . Change your name and counter on the main screen.

Roll it

Swipe to roll and play wherever it lands. Every face scores: win 3, draw 1 each, lose −1. Most points takes the level, and a tie goes to a sudden-death face.

Score: your face points × 100, plus 1,000 for clearing the level, all × the level number.

    Stack it

    Turn the big cube and make your line through it. You always get the first move.

    Score: 1,000 for clearing the level, plus 10 for every empty square left, all × the level number.
